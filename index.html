What Enveloop does with your Google data

Enveloop runs inside Google Docs and Sheets and only touches the files you point it at, while you are using it. Here is exactly what it reads and writes, and why.

Reads the spreadsheet you choose

Enveloop reads the sheet you select so it can turn each row into one label. It uses the header row as merge fields, and reads the data only when you run a merge.

Writes your labels into a Google Doc

In Google Sheets, Enveloop creates a new document containing the finished labels. In Google Docs, it lays the labels out in the document you currently have open, replacing its contents.

Opens the label template you pick

Each label format is a document that defines the page size and grid. Enveloop opens the one you select, read-only, to copy that geometry so the labels line up with the physical sheet.

Shows the add-on panel

Enveloop needs permission to display its own sidebar inside Google Docs and Sheets. That is what the panel you work in is built from.

Identifies your account

Enveloop reads the email address of the signed-in Google account to recognise you and apply your licence. It is not used for anything else.

Your label data stays in Google

The merge itself runs inside Google Apps Script, so the contents of your spreadsheet and labels are never sent to an Enveloop server. See our privacy policy for the full detail.
