Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Enveloop ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Enveloop add-on for Google Docs and Google Sheets, or visit enveloop.io (together, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: such as your name and email address, associated with your Google account when you install or sign in to the add-on.
  • Document and Spreadsheet Access: to generate labels, the add-on requests permission to display dialogs and sidebars, and to read and write the specific Google Doc or Sheet you are actively working in. Enveloop only uses this access to build the label document you request — it does not scan, index, or retain the content of your sheets or docs beyond the merge operation itself.
  • Subscription Status: the add-on makes an external request to verify your subscription/license status so paid features can be unlocked.
  • Device and Usage Data: such as browser type, general usage patterns, and basic error logs to help us maintain and improve the Service.
  • Payment Information: paid plans are processed by our third-party payment provider, Stripe. Enveloop does not receive or store your full payment card details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service.
  • To generate the labels and documents you request through the add-on.
  • To verify subscription status and unlock paid features.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate with you.
  • To monitor and improve the Service's performance and reliability.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Enveloop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only access the Google Docs and Sheets content necessary to perform the mail-merge and label-generation features you request, and we do not use that content for advertising, and do not allow humans to read it except as necessary to provide support you have requested or as required by law.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics on our website to understand traffic and usage trends, and we may use other third-party service providers to help operate the Service, such as cloud hosting and payment processing. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their services and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption in transit. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain account and subscription information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. We do not retain the content of your Google Docs or Sheets beyond the time needed to complete a requested merge operation. You may request deletion of your data at any time as described below.

Your Rights and Account Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. You can remove the add-on from your Google account at any time from your Google account's connected apps settings. If you need help, contact us at support@enveloop.io and we will process your request within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own. By using the Service, you consent to the transfer of information to countries that may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@enveloop.io.
