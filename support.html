Frequently asked questions

How do I install Enveloop?

Enveloop will be available to install directly from within Google Docs or Sheets. We're finishing up before launch — reach out and we'll let you know as soon as it's ready.

Which label sheets does Enveloop support?

Enveloop is designed to work with popular label sheet formats, including many Avery-compatible sizes. We're expanding template coverage ahead of launch.

Is my spreadsheet data safe?

Enveloop only accesses the document and spreadsheet data needed to generate your labels. We do not store or share the content of your sheets. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Yes — contact support@enveloop.io within 30 days of purchase and we'll process a refund per our Terms of Service.

Do you offer team or bulk licensing?

Yes, contact support@enveloop.io and we'll set up licensing for your team.
